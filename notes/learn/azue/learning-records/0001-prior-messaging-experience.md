# Prior knowledge: messaging fundamentals and experience with other brokers

User has hands-on experience with RabbitMQ, Kafka, and AWS SQS. They understand core messaging concepts: queues, publish/subscribe, competing consumers, message acknowledgement, and at-least-once vs. at-most-once delivery semantics. They are comfortable with distributed systems patterns.

**Implications**: Lessons should skip messaging fundamentals and focus on what makes Service Bus unique. Comparisons to RabbitMQ/Kafka/SQS are useful anchoring points. Code examples can assume familiarity with async/await patterns.
