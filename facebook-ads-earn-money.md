Here’s the full playbook broken down into actionable steps:

**Step 1: Pick a niche and find clients** 

Start with **local service businesses** that have never run ads — roofers, plumbers, HVAC, landscapers, etc. They’re easy to find and have high customer lifetime value.

- Search Google Maps for businesses in your area with few or no reviews (they’re likely not advertising)
- Cold call, email, or walk in and offer to run their ads for a flat monthly retainer ($2K–$5K) or a per-lead fee
- Your pitch: “I’ll get you booked appointments. You just show up.”

**Step 2: Find proven winning ads in Facebook Ad Library** 

Go to **facebook.com/ads/library** and search for ads in your client’s industry (e.g. “roofing”, “roof repair”).

- Filter by **location** (target the client’s region or similar markets)
- Look for ads that have been **running 6+ months** — long-running ads are profitable ads, otherwise the advertiser would have killed them
- Save the ones with strong hooks, clear offers, and good visuals — these are your templates
- Note the format (video vs. static), the script structure, and the call-to-action

**Step 3: Recreate the ads with AI using Higgsfield** 

Go to **higgsfield.ai** — it’s an AI video generation platform that can rebuild video ads.

- Upload or reference the winning ad concepts you found
- Swap in your **client’s branding** (logo, colors, business name, phone number)
- Use Higgsfield’s tools to generate new video creatives that follow the same proven structure but are original to your client

You’re not copying ads verbatim — you’re using proven frameworks and rebuilding them with AI.

**Step 4: Write the scripts and ad copy with Claude** 

Use **Claude** (claude.ai) to write:

- **Video scripts** — feed it the structure of the winning ad and ask it to write a version for your client’s business, location, and offer
- **Ad copy** — the headline, primary text, and description that appear alongside the video on Facebook/Instagram
- **Variations** — ask for 3–5 versions of each so you can test different hooks and angles

In one afternoon you can produce **10–15 complete video ads** per client.

**Step 5: Launch the ads on Meta** 

Set up a **Meta Business Manager** account and create ad campaigns for your client.

- Use **lead generation** as your campaign objective
- Target the client’s **local service area** (radius around their city)
- Target homeowners, relevant age demographics, and interest-based audiences
- Attach a **simple lead form** (name, phone, address, “when do you need service?”) — keep it short, 3–4 fields max
- Start with a modest daily budget ($20–$50/day) and scale what works

**Step 6: Set up an AI texting bot to follow up on leads** 

This is the critical piece — **speed to lead** determines whether the lead converts. Set up an AI-powered SMS bot that:

- **Texts every lead within 2 minutes** of form submission
- Qualifies them with a few quick questions
- **Books them directly onto the client’s calendar** (integrate with Calendly, Cal.com, or Google Calendar)

Tools to do this:

- **GoHighLevel (GHL)** — the most popular option for local marketing agencies; has built-in CRM, SMS automation, AI bot, and calendar booking
- **n8n + Twilio** — more DIY; use n8n to automate the workflow and Twilio for SMS
- **Zapier + an AI chatbot** — connect Meta lead forms → Zapier → your SMS/booking tool

**Step 7: Deliver results and retain clients** 

- The roofer wakes up to **booked appointments on their calendar** with zero effort
- Send the client a **weekly report** showing leads generated, appointments booked, and cost per lead
- Bill monthly — either a **flat retainer** ($2K–$5K/month) or **per qualified lead** ($50–$150 each depending on the trade)

You’re combining free research (Ad Library), AI video generation (Higgsfield), AI copywriting (Claude), and AI lead follow-up into a fully automated service. Once it’s running for a client, maintenance is minimal — which is why some clients pay the same fee for years.

**Meta Business Manager (business.facebook.com)** 

You create **one Business Manager account** with your personal Facebook login, and from there you can manage **unlimited client ad accounts** separately. Each client gets their own:

- **Ad account** — so budgets, billing, and campaigns are isolated
- **Facebook Page** connection — you request access to the client’s page (or create one for them)
- **Payment method** — the client can add their own credit card, so ad spend bills directly to them

**How to set it up** 

1. Go to **business.facebook.com** and create your Business Manager
2. For each new client, go to **Business Settings → Accounts → Ad Accounts → Add**
3. Choose **“Create a new ad account”** — name it after the client (e.g., “Smith Roofing”)
4. Assign yourself as the admin on that ad account
5. Under **Payment Settings** for that ad account, add the client’s payment method so they pay for ad spend directly
6. Request access to (or create) their Facebook Page so ads run under their business name

**Key things to know** 

- **You never share your login with clients.** You manage everything; they just see results
- **Each ad account is firewalled.** If one account has an issue, it doesn’t affect the others
- **You can add team members later** if you scale and hire help — just give them access to specific accounts
- **Billing is per ad account**, so client A’s spend never mixes with client B’s
- There’s a **limit of ~5 ad accounts initially**, but Meta raises this as your spend history grows (you can request increases)

This is the standard setup that every agency and freelance media buyer uses. One login, many clients, everything clean and separated.
